Why Fun Fair Uganda

Built Around Your Flight, Your Schedule and Your Peace of Mind

Flight Delay Tracking

We track your flight live. If your flight lands early or is delayed, your driver adjusts the pickup time at no extra charge, and the first 60 minutes of waiting after landing are free.

100% Fixed Transparent Pricing

No surprise surge pricing or hidden toll charges. The all-inclusive rate you see when booking is the rate you pay.

24/7 Human Dispatch Support

Need to adjust pickup times or change destinations? Our local dispatch desk responds instantly via WhatsApp and phone calls.
