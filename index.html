Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking your pickup

Our driver will monitor your flight status and meet you inside the Entebbe International Airport arrival hall holding a paging board with your full name. They will assist you with luggage and guide you directly to your reserved vehicle.
No need to worry! We track all inbound flights in real-time using your provided flight number. Your driver will adjust their arrival time accordingly without any extra waiting charges.
We accept cash in US Dollars (USD) or Uganda Shillings (UGX) upon arrival. We also accept local Mobile Money (MTN / Airtel) and pre-arranged bank wire transfers for corporate accounts.
