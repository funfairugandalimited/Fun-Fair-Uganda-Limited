# Fun Fair Uganda Limited — production deployment

## Recommended architecture
- Domain: funfairugandalimited.co.ug
- Source/backups: GitHub repository (private or public)
- Hosting/CDN/SSL: Cloudflare Pages
- Search indexing: Google Search Console + Google Business Profile

## Upload
Deploy the contents of this folder as a static site. `index.html` is the homepage.

## DNS
Add the domain to Cloudflare and follow Cloudflare Pages' Custom Domain setup. Use the exact DNS/nameserver values shown in the Cloudflare dashboard; do not guess them.

## SEO
After the domain is live:
1. Verify the domain in Google Search Console.
2. Submit https://funfairugandalimited.co.ug/sitemap.xml
3. Inspect https://funfairugandalimited.co.ug/ and request indexing.
4. Create/claim the official Google Business Profile for Fun Fair Uganda Limited.
5. Keep the business name, phone number, address and website consistent across the Business Profile and other legitimate business listings.

## Important
Google indexing is not instantaneous and no legitimate setup can guarantee that a search for the generic word "funfair" alone will always return the company first. The goal is to establish the brand query "Fun Fair Uganda" and the local service queries around airport transfers/chauffeur services.
