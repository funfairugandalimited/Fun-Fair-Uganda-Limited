## Google Search Console
1. Verify `funfairugandalimited.co.ug` in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://funfairugandalimited.co.ug/sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect `https://funfairugandalimited.co.ug/` and request indexing.
4. Create/claim the official Google Business Profile for Fun Fair Uganda Limited.
5. Keep the business name, phone number, address and website consistent across legitimate business listings.

Google indexing is not instantaneous, and no legitimate SEO setup can guarantee first place for the generic query `funfair`. The strongest target is the branded query `Fun Fair Uganda` plus local service searches such as `Entebbe airport transfer`, `Kampala chauffeur`, and `airport transfer Uganda`.
